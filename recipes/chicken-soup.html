Chicken Soup

Description

Chicken soup is a warm and comforting dish made with chicken, vegetables, and broth. It is easy to prepare and great for cold days.

This recipe has a light but flavorful broth and soft vegetables, making it a simple homemade meal.

Ingredients

  • Chicken
  • Chicken broth
  • Carrots
  • Celery
  • Onion
  • Garlic
  • Noodles or rice
  • Salt and pepper
  • Parsley

Steps

  1. Place chicken, broth, onion, and garlic in a pot.
  2. Bring the soup to a boil.
  3. Reduce the heat and let it simmer.
  4. Add carrots and celery.
  5. Cook until the chicken and vegetables are tender.
  6. Add noodles or rice and cook until soft.
  7. Season with salt, pepper, and parsley before serving.
