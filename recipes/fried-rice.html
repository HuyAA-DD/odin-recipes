Fried Rice

Description

Fried rice is a quick and tasty dish made with cooked rice, vegetables, eggs, and seasoning. It is simple to make and works well with leftover rice.

This dish is popular because it is flexible. You can add meat, seafood, or extra vegetables depending on what you have.

Ingredients

  • Cooked rice
  • Eggs
  • Carrots
  • Peas
  • Green onions
  • Soy sauce
  • Garlic
  • Cooking oil
  • Salt and pepper

Steps

  1. Heat oil in a pan or wok.
  2. Add garlic and cook until fragrant.
  3. Add vegetables and stir-fry for a few minutes.
  4. Push the vegetables aside and scramble the eggs.
  5. Add cooked rice and mix everything together.
  6. Add soy sauce, salt, and pepper.
  7. Stir-fry until the rice is hot and evenly mixed.
