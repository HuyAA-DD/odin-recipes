Lasagna

Description

Lasagna is a classic Italian dish made with layers of pasta, meat sauce, cheese, and tomato sauce. It is rich, filling, and perfect for family meals.

This recipe uses simple ingredients and creates a warm, cheesy dish with great flavor.

Ingredients

  • Lasagna noodles
  • Ground beef
  • Tomato sauce
  • Ricotta cheese
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Parmesan cheese
  • Onion
  • Garlic
  • Salt and pepper

Steps

  1. Cook the lasagna noodles according to the package instructions.
  2. Cook the ground beef with onion and garlic.
  3. Add tomato sauce and season with salt and pepper.
  4. Layer noodles, meat sauce, and cheese in a baking dish.
  5. Repeat the layers until all ingredients are used.
  6. Bake in the oven until the cheese is melted and golden.
  7. Let it cool slightly before serving.
